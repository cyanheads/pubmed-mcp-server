# 2.12.4 — 2026-11-24

## Changed

- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` declares `blank_filter` alone in `errors[]`** ([#319](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/319)) — the NCBI reasons it listed can no longer end a call. A documentation-only change to the advertised error list; `reason` stays `type: string`.

## Fixed

- **A failed or hung PMC ID Converter lookup no longer fails `pubmed_fetch_fulltext`** ([#319](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/319)) — the ids it was resolving carry a `pmc` `service-error` naming the ID Converter and go on to Europe PMC and Unpaywall, and the other lookup's answer is kept. While a later tier can run, the lookups take half the call deadline.
- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` prints each tier `detail` in full on the `chain:` and `Not queried:` lines** ([#318](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/318)) — Markdown-escaped, as `structuredContent` carries it; the `<upstream>` URL replacement is gone, and URLs are redacted at the source.
- **`pubmed_find_related` marks each coverage failure `retryable` or `not retryable` in its `content[]` trailer** ([#318](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/318)) — when the set is mixed, the notice's retry clause names only the retryable providers.
- **NCBI single-attempt mode arms only after a call made as many attempts as half of `NCBI_TOTAL_DEADLINE_MS` always fits, or all its retries allowed** ([#320](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/320)) — 5 attempts at defaults. A later chain step's short deadline share no longer puts its endpoint on one attempt per call.
- **`pubmed_europepmc_search` POSTs a composed query over 2,000 characters to `/rest/searchPOST`** ([#321](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/321)) — a shorter one keeps the identical GET. An HTTP 413 or 414 ends as non-retryable `europepmc_invalid_input` with a hint to split the query, and `pubmed_europepmc_search` and `pubmed_europepmc_fetch` advertise that case in the reason's `when`.
