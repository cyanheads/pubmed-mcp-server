# 2.12.1 — 2026-11-11

## Added

- **`pubmed_lookup_mesh` records carry `recordType` and `searchTerm`** ([#282](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/282), [#294](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/294)) — descriptors, supplementary concepts, qualifiers, and pharmacological actions each name their MeSH record type and the PubMed clause that searches them (`"Name"[mh]`, `[nm]`, `[pa]`, `[pt]`, `[sh]`). A supplementary concept such as dapagliflozin is searched as `[nm]`, which no `[mh]` clause matches.
- **`pubmed_lookup_mesh` `includeDetails` adds `yearIntroduced`, `previousIndexing`, and `subheadings`** ([#296](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/296)) — `subheadings` is the allowable qualifiers, on the exact match. Look each `previousIndexing` heading up by name: a retired heading matches nothing as `[mh]`.
- **`pubmed_find_related` rows carry `doi`, `pmcId`, `pmcUrl`, `pubmedUrl`, and `epubDate`** ([#297](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/297), [#293](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/293)) — the row shape search summaries use, and each `content[]` item opens with its PubMed link, then its DOI and PMC link when it has them. Search summaries gain `epubDate`, and their `pubmedUrl`, which every row already set, is now declared required.
- **`pubmed_find_related` accepts `relation` as an alias of `relationship`** ([#290](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/290)).

## Changed

- **`pubmed_lookup_mesh` pins the record the query names** ([#282](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/282)) — the exact match reads the literal heading as well as NCBI's translated one, and a descriptor ranks over a same-named qualifier or pharmacological-action record. `Randomized Controlled Trial` and `Drug Therapy` previously pinned "Randomized Controlled Trials as Topic" and the qualifier.
- **Summary `pubDate` is ISO 8601 at PubMed's stated precision** ([#293](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/293)) — a month- or year-only date is no longer padded to a day, a range reads `start/end`, and a range with equal ends reads as its point (`2026 Sep-Sep` is `2026-09`). Existing `pubDate` values change content on `pubmed_search_articles` and `pubmed_find_related`. `pubmed_lookup_citation` accepts either end of a range in its year check, and its `year_mismatch` message names both.
- **`pubmed_search_articles` unmatched-clause notice advises by the clause's field** ([#294](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/294)) — a MeSH clause points to `pubmed_lookup_mesh`, a journal to its NLM abbreviation or ISSN, a language to its English name or three-letter code. The `sort`, `journal`, `meshTerms`, and `language` descriptions state their value forms.
- **Outage recoveries name the other route** ([#288](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/288)) — `pubmed_search_articles` points to `pubmed_europepmc_search` with `sources: ["MED"]`, `pubmed_europepmc_search` to `pubmed_search_articles`, and `pubmed_europepmc_fetch` to `pubmed_fetch_articles` for `MED` records. The shared `europepmc_unreachable` recovery no longer names NCBI PMC and Unpaywall.
- **After an E-utility endpoint fails on every retry, later calls to it make one attempt until one succeeds** ([#287](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/287)) — other endpoints keep their retry ladder. The `ncbi_unreachable` message says when a call was not retried.
- **Article links in `content[]` render as CommonMark autolinks** ([#307](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/307)) — the PubMed, PMC, Bookshelf, OA copy, and Unpaywall links on `pubmed_search_articles`, `pubmed_fetch_articles`, and `pubmed_fetch_fulltext`, and `**URL:**` on `pubmed_europepmc_search` and `pubmed_europepmc_fetch`. `structuredContent` is unchanged.
- **`pubmed-guide` skill covers the new MeSH fields** — its gates search a record's `searchTerm` (`"Name"[nm]` for a supplementary concept), take a `Heading/qualifier` clause's qualifier from `subheadings`, look up `previousIndexing` headings for a search dated before `yearIntroduced`, and follow the notice's advice by field.

## Fixed

- **`pubmed_search_articles` `sort: "journal"` is honored** ([#291](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/291)) — it was sent as a key ESearch does not have and returned the default order.
- **A page that reaches PubMed's 9,999-record window says so in `notice`** ([#292](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/292)) — it gives the matches no `offset` reaches and advises splitting the query by `dateRange`.
- **`pubmed_europepmc_search` no longer blames an HTTP 503 on a documented sort** ([#289](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/289)) — such a 503 is retried as an outage instead of failing as `europepmc_invalid_input`.
- **PMC ID Converter error responses no longer carry the converter's error page in client-facing error data.**
