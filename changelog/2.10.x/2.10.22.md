# 2.10.22 — 2026-10-12

## Added

- **Author `suffix`** ([#226](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/226)) — `pubmed_fetch_articles` authors, investigators, and book editors, and `pubmed_fetch_fulltext` authors carry the generational suffix verbatim (`Jr`, `2nd`, `IV`). Every citation style prints it, and full-text references keep it.
- **`pubmed_fetch_articles` notice block** ([#237](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/237)) — a retracted, corrected, or questioned article opens with the same notice lines as `pubmed_format_citations`, directly under its title.

## Changed

- **`pubmed_spell_check` corrects in place** ([#241](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/241)) — `corrected` is the caller's query with ESpell's replacements applied, so field tags, operators, spacing, and words in capitals or with digits (gene symbols) stay as written. `hasSuggestion` is true only when a replacement was applied.
- **`pubmed_lookup_mesh` IDs** ([#238](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/238)) — `meshId` is the MeSH unique ID from eSummary 2.0 (`D…`, `C…`, `Q…`), including records the Entrez UID does not encode. It is omitted when eSummary returns no record for the UID.
- **`pubmed_lookup_mesh` pinning** ([#247](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/247)) — an exact heading that matches a pharmacological-action record pins its descriptor instead; `treeNumbers` leaves out the record's own UI.
- **Deferral notices** ([#236](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/236)) — `pubmed_fetch_articles`, `pubmed_fetch_fulltext`, and `pubmed_europepmc_fetch` share one notice naming the ceiling for re-calling the deferred items and the one for repeating the call. `nextDeferredCharacters` is described as the continuation ceiling.
- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` ordering** ([#235](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/235)) — `articles` follow routing order (`pmcids`, then `pmids`, then `dois`, request order within each), so a `maxResponseCharacters` cut is the same on every call.
- **Europe PMC `sort`** ([#239](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/239)) — `relevance` is sent as no sort, `FIRST_IDATE_D` joins the documented fields, and a sort without a field plus `asc`/`desc` per key fails as `europepmc_invalid_input` before any request. A second HTTP 503 to a sorted search that the unsorted query survives names the sort.
- **Europe PMC `source` case** ([#244](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/244)) — `source` and `sources` accept lower-case codes (`med`); search hint text and server instructions show `records: [{ source, epmcId }]`.
- **Framework 0.13.15 adoption** — `framework-skills/` sync.

## Fixed

- **Citation journal names** ([#227](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/227)) — APA and MLA drop NLM's place, year, and edition qualifier (`Lancet (London, England)` → `Lancet`) and keep an edition the ISO abbreviation also names.
- **Citation page ranges** ([#228](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/228)) — BibTeX `pages` writes each range in full, and RIS `SP`/`EP` take the first and last page of a list (`1308, 1310-4` → `1308`/`1314`).
- **Article numbers** ([#231](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/231)) — a dashless page that equals the locator with its hyphens removed (`e0364621` beside `e03646-21`) cites as the locator.
- **Citation whitespace** ([#233](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/233)) — line breaks inside titles, names, keywords, and abstracts collapse to one space in every style, so RIS stays one tagged line per value. `pubmed_format_citations` escapes Markdown in the title line.
- **Literal `None` page** ([#248](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/248)) — NCBI's `None` placeholder is no longer read as a page.
- **Invisible characters** ([#224](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/224)) — U+FEFF, U+00AD, and U+200B inside words are dropped from PMC and Europe PMC full-text XML before parsing, ending split words (`hepatopulmonary s yndrome`).
- **Space before punctuation** ([#225](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/225)) — a line break between a formula or other element and the closing punctuation after it no longer leaves a space (`L<0 .` → `L<0.`).
- **MathML `<semantics>`** ([#230](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/230)) — reads one rendering of the expression, never the TeX, MathType, or Content annotations beside it.
- **Body footnotes** ([#229](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/229)) — a `<fn>` or `<fn-group>` in a body section reads one paragraph per footnote, as it does in back matter.
- **Table and asset labels** ([#240](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/240)) — one trailing `.`, `|`, or `:` is dropped (`Table 1.` → `Table 1`), and a bare-number label is named with its type in `omittedAssetNames` and asset headings (`Supplementary material 1`).
- **Rendered dates** ([#242](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/242)) — a one-digit numeric month or day is zero-padded in the rendered date; the structured parts stay as deposited.
