# 2.10.20 — 2026-10-04

## Added

- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` takes `pmcids`, `pmids`, and `dois` together** ([#192](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/192)) — up to 10 distinct identifiers across the three; an article several of them name is fetched and returned once. `deferred.idType` is now optional: a deferral spanning fields lists its ids under `deferred.pmcids` / `pmids` / `dois`.
- **`pubmed_europepmc_fetch` `maxResponseCharacters`** ([#188](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/188)) — opt-in ceiling for the whole response; records past it are deferred whole, listed in `deferred.records`, and flagged `truncated: true`.
- **`pubmed_search_articles` `maxResults: 0`** ([#191](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/191)) — a count-only search returning `totalCount` alone.
- **Input aliases** ([#190](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/190)) — E-utilities and sibling-tool parameter names (`retmax`, `retstart`, `term`, `pageSize`, `maxResults`, and others) are rewritten to each tool's canonical key on `pubmed_search_articles`, `pubmed_lookup_mesh`, `pubmed_find_related`, `pubmed_europepmc_search`, and `pubmed_spell_check`.
- **Real-article test corpus** — `tests/corpus/` runs openly licensed PMC and PubMed records against reviewed expected output under `bun run test`; `bun run test:corpus`, `bun run corpus:snapshot`, and `scripts/corpus-add.ts` maintain it.

## Changed

- **Input tightening, deliberate:** `pubmed_lookup_citation` rejects a blank or whitespace-only `journal`, `year`, `volume`, `firstPage`, or `authorName`, and a `year` that is not four digits, with -32602 naming the field ([#187](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/187)). Some of these used to return a match, such as a whitespace `year` beside a full journal citation.
- **Input tightening, deliberate:** `pubmed_fetch_fulltext` rejects a `sections` element of only whitespace or invisible characters with `blank_filter`, and trims surrounding spaces from the terms it keeps ([#186](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/186)). An exactly-empty element is skipped.
- **PMC ID Converter HTTP 400** surfaces as `invalidParams` (-32602) instead of -32007.
- **`pubmed_search_articles` `dateRange`** — the `minDate`/`maxDate` descriptions say an empty bound drops the whole range, and the partial-range notice suggests the open lower bound `minDate: "1000"` instead of `"1800"`.
- **Zero-fit deferral notice** — `pubmed_fetch_articles`, `pubmed_fetch_fulltext`, and `pubmed_europepmc_fetch` name the first record as the one that exceeds `maxResponseCharacters`.
- **Output descriptions** — `pubmed_fetch_articles` `grants[].acronym` names the funding institute, never the grant; `pubmed_lookup_citation` describes its citation as needing a journal or a four-digit year.
- **`server.json` npm entries** launch the package bin through `npx` without the `run start:*` arguments, and the `streamable-http` entry sets `MCP_TRANSPORT_TYPE` to `http`.
- **`.mcpb` bundle** — `.mcpbignore` excludes the build-only root directories `/src/`, `/scripts/`, `/tests/`, `/docs/`, `/coverage/`, `/.github/`, `/.vscode/`, and `/.cache/`, so the bundle drops from 11.7 MB to 10.7 MB and carries no corpus fixtures. The scaffold gap is tracked in [cyanheads/mcp-ts-core#508](https://github.com/cyanheads/mcp-ts-core/issues/508).
- **Framework 0.13.11 adoption** — the Docker image prunes musl-only packages (`scripts/prune-musl-packages.ts`); devcheck's TODO and tracked-secret scans, `lint:packaging` check 16, per-language CodeQL jobs, `.env*` ignore rules, and the `framework-skills/` sync follow the framework.
- **Docs** — the README MCP SDK badge reads `^2.2.0` (was `^2.1.0`), and the `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md` Commands table gains the corpus commands.

## Fixed

- **Europe PMC text** ([#181](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/181), [#195](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/195)) — entity-encoded formatting tags (`&lt;i&gt;`) are stripped; a formatting tag right after `.`, `:`, `!`, or `?` reads as a space, so bold or italic abstract headings no longer fuse into the next sentence; PubMed's `<or=` notation is kept as text.
- **`openalex_invalid_response`** survives exhausted retries instead of being re-stamped `openalex_unreachable` ([#182](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/182)).
- **Bookshelf chapter dates** ([#189](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/189)) — `pubmed_format_citations` dates a chapter by its last revision, else its contribution, never the book's start year; Vancouver prints both, RIS adds `DA`, and a book still being updated prints an open range (`2000-`).
- **Bookshelf chapter summaries** ([#199](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/199)) — `pubDate` on `pubmed_search_articles` and `pubmed_find_related` chapter rows, and the `pubmed_lookup_citation` verification date, come from the chapter's own ESummary `DocDate`.
- **`pubmed_fetch_articles` Article Dates** — `content[]` zero-pads them for every record (`Electronic 2021-6-8` → `2021-06-08`); `structuredContent` is unchanged.
- **Nested inline markup** in PubMed titles, abstracts, and keywords — nested `<sup>`/`<sub>`, emphasis inside scripts, and MathML — no longer deletes text or fuses words ([#211](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/211)).
- **Collective authors** keep their affiliations ([#212](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/212)).
- **Absent journal fields** are omitted rather than returned as `""` ([#213](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/213)); for a record with no ISO journal abbreviation, `pubmed_format_citations` Vancouver now prints the full journal title where nothing printed before.
- **A grant with no `GrantID`** shows its acronym beside the agency rather than as the ID ([#214](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/214)).
- **`pubmed_lookup_citation`** — a journal-less citation ECitMatch cannot match no longer drops every later citation; the rest are re-requested within the call's one deadline and retry budget ([#193](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/193)).
- **`pubmed_lookup_citation`** reads an integer `year` in a lone citation object as its decimal string ([#198](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/198)), and verification reads ESummary 2.0, so consortium authors join the roster ([#201](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/201)).
- **JATS table cells and footnotes** keep their line breaks as `\n`, one line per footnote with its label leading, and inline markup is never spaced (`0.108a`); `content[]` joins those lines with ` · ` ([#185](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/185)).
- **JATS affiliations** inside `<contrib-group>` or `<contrib>` are read ([#196](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/196)), and `content[]` lists them as `- ` bullets instead of a numbered list, so a deposited label isn't numbered twice.
- **Adjacent citation markers** read `[1,2,3,4]`, not `[1234]` ([#197](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/197)).
- **Nested lists** sit below their parent item, and prose after a nested list keeps its place ([#200](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/200), [#203](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/203)).
- **Abstracts** — a list starts on its own line ([#202](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/202)), text beside a `<sec>` is kept ([#204](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/204)), and a section title with its own closing punctuation gains no second colon ([#205](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/205)).
- **Institution ids** (ROR, GRID, Funder DOIs) no longer fuse onto institution names in affiliations and funding statements ([#208](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/208)).
- **References** — unseparated author names gain `, `, an empty `<etal/>` reads `et al.`, page ranges read `56–71`, and volume and issue `16(1)`; `references[].label` drops its enclosing brackets (`[19]` → `19`) ([#209](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/209)).
- **Boxed text** opens with a `[Box: …]` line and closes with `[End of box]` ([#210](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/210)).
