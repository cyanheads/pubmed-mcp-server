# 2.10.21 — 2026-10-09

## Added

- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` back matter** ([#206](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/206)) — on the PMC tier, every `<back>` child except `<ref-list>` (acknowledgments, notes and declarations, footnotes, glossary, appendices) becomes a `sections[]` entry after the body, titled by its element when it carries no title or label. `maxSections`, `maxCharacters`, and `sections` treat it as ordinary sections; `maxCharacters` serves it after the body, tables, and assets.
- **`pubmed_format_citations` `notices`** ([#215](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/215)) — each `citations[]` entry lists the retraction, erratum, and expression-of-concern notices NCBI links to the article, and `content[]` shows them ahead of the citations, opening with a "Retracted" warning when one is a retraction. Vancouver notes each `RetractionIn` and `ErratumIn` after the DOI, and APA adds a retraction parenthetical; MLA, BibTeX, and RIS are unchanged.
- **`pubmed_fetch_articles` `includeInvestigators`** ([#216](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/216)) — opt-in `investigators[]` with the members NCBI lists for a collective author, uncapped (PMID 33933206 lists 6,349); their affiliations follow the authors' in `affiliations`.
- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` singular id keys** ([#221](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/221)) — `pmcid`, `pmcId`, `pmid`, and `doi` are accepted as aliases of `pmcids`, `pmids`, and `dois`, and a lone string reads as a one-element array.
- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` `overLimit`** ([#222](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/222)) — a call may name up to 50 distinct identifiers; the first 10 are fetched and the rest come back unfetched in `overLimit` (with `truncated: true` and a notice) for a follow-up call.

## Changed

- **Output-only ceilings take no upper bound** ([#223](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/223)) — `maxSections`, `maxCharacters`, `maxCharactersPerSection`, and `maxResponseCharacters` on `pubmed_fetch_fulltext`, and `maxResponseCharacters` on `pubmed_fetch_articles` and `pubmed_europepmc_fetch`, lose their `.max()`; a large value now means no limit instead of -32602. `tools/list` advertises the safe-integer bound.
- **Error data `url` → `host`** — NCBI external-request and Unpaywall content-fetch errors carry `data.host` where they carried `data.url`. Log keys follow: the Unpaywall `version` is `oaVersion`, and the NCBI init record's `hasApiKey` is `ncbiKeyConfigured`.
- **Framework 0.13.14 adoption** — `framework-skills/` sync, and the antipattern check's patterns use POSIX space classes.
- **Tests** — assertions across the suites rewritten to pin exact outputs, so each can fail on a regression; the fuzz harness reports what each phase drove through the handler.
- **Docs** — the `CITATION.cff` abstract names Europe PMC and Vancouver.

## Fixed

- **MathML-only formulas** ([#207](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/207)) — `pubmed_fetch_fulltext` renders them in TeX-style linear form (`∑_{j=1}^{N}l_{ij}`, matrix cells split by `&` and `\\`) instead of fused leaf text. Formulas with a `<tex-math>` are unchanged.
- **`pubmed_format_citations` APA and MLA** ([#217](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/217)) — journal titles are title-cased (names like `eLife` and `JAMA` untouched), APA page ranges use an en dash with the elided end expanded, a page equal to the record's article locator prints as `Article <n>` / `art. <n>`, and BibTeX and RIS drop keywords that repeat an earlier one ignoring case.
- **Bullet-character abstract paragraphs** ([#218](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/218)) — a run of paragraphs opening with `•`, `‣`, `⁃`, or `◦` reads one per line, as a `<list>` does.
- **References** ([#219](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/219)) — source indentation no longer leaves a space before `.`, `,`, `;`, `:`, `)`, or `]` (`Henry L .` → `Henry L.`).
- **Reference rendering** — the per-`<pub-id>` prefix check reads a bounded window of the citation rather than all of it, so rendering is linear in the citation's length.

## Security

- **Upstream fetch failures name only the host** — errors, `error.data`, and log records for NCBI, Europe PMC, OpenAlex, and Unpaywall requests carry the upstream host, never the request URL or the API key and contact email its query string carries.
