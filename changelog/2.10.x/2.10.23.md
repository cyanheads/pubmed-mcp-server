# 2.10.23 — 2026-10-23

## Added

- **`pubmed_fetch_articles` `dataBanks`** ([#257](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/257)) — each journal article lists its `DataBankList` as `{ name, accessionNumbers? }`, verbatim and in NCBI's order, on both surfaces: trial registration IDs, GenBank and PDB accessions, Dryad DOIs. `pubmed_search_articles` with `<accession>[si]` finds other records citing one.
- **`pubmed_format_citations` `styles` alias** ([#254](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/254)) — resolves to `format`; not advertised in `inputSchema`, and sent beside `format` it is rejected.
- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` `maxCharsPerArticle` alias** ([#258](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/258)) — resolves to `maxCharacters`; not advertised in `inputSchema`, and sent beside `maxCharacters` it is rejected.

## Changed

- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` `overflowMode: "outline"`** ([#252](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/252)) — a section's budget is split evenly between its own text and each subsection subtree, at every level, so later subsections and back matter keep excerpts. A part whose share would cut it under 100 characters (or `maxCharactersPerSection`, when lower) returns heading-only and its share goes to the others.
- **Citation formatting** ([#253](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/253)) — Vancouver writes page ranges in elided Citing Medicine form (`1637-1645` → `1637-45`, `S12-S15` → `S12-5`); BibTeX `issn` and RIS `SN` print the journal's linking ISSN; RIS `TI` drops its trailing period.
- **`content[]` Markdown escaping** ([#255](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/255)) — upstream text rendered by `pubmed_fetch_fulltext`, `pubmed_fetch_articles`, `pubmed_europepmc_fetch`, `pubmed_europepmc_search`, `pubmed_search_articles`, `pubmed_find_related`, `pubmed_lookup_citation`, `pubmed_lookup_mesh`, `pubmed_convert_ids`, and Vancouver citations is escaped, so measurements, HGVS variants, and formulae render as written. The caller's query in `pubmed_search_articles`, `pubmed_spell_check`, `pubmed_lookup_mesh`, and the Europe PMC cursors is a code span, and BibTeX and RIS fences outlast any backtick run inside them. `structuredContent` is unchanged.

## Fixed

- **`pubmed_spell_check` field tags and single letters** ([#250](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/250)) — a replacement overlapping a field tag keeps the caller's text (`risk[tiab] AND alzhiemer` no longer becomes `[tia]`), and one letter replaced by another (`q fever` → `c fever`) is kept; a letter grown into a word still applies.
- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` consortium authors** ([#251](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/251)) — `collectiveName` is the `<collab>`'s own name, without nested member lists or footnote markers; the same holds for collabs in reference citations.
- **Europe PMC bare `<NAME>` text** ([#256](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/256)) — a bare tag that is neither a formatting or void name nor closed later in the string stays as text (`HuMab<CD20>`), in `structuredContent` and `content[]`.
- **EFetch double-encoded entities** ([#255](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/255)) — titles, abstracts, affiliations, keywords, and book titles decode the one entity layer NCBI double-encodes (`R&amp;amp;D` → `R&D`).
- **`pubmed_fetch_fulltext` preformatted blocks** ([#255](https://github.com/cyanheads/pubmed-mcp-server/issues/255)) — the fence is longer than any backtick run in the block, so its content cannot close it early.
